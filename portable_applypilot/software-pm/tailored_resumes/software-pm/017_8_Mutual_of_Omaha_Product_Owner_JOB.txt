**WHAT YOU'LL DO:**



* Drive Product Solutions: Lead cross\-functional teams and Agile Release Train resources to deliver high\-value product solutions aligned with strategic visions, ensuring consistent customer value delivery.
* Backlog Prioritization: Manage and refine product backlogs by balancing business value, stakeholder input, and strategic priorities to achieve impactful outcomes.
* Stakeholder Collaboration: Partner with ART leadership, business owners, subject matter experts, and other Product Owners to define and refine features, ensuring clear acceptance criteria and seamless communication across teams.
* Agile Leadership: Act as a Product Owner for an Agile Release Train car, participating in planning, demos, retrospectives, and ceremonies to enhance product quality and team velocity.
* Outcome Monitoring: Oversee business outcomes by tracking progress, adjusting priorities, and resolving delivery challenges while ensuring seamless transitions to operations, including training and communication.
* Risk Mitigation: Identify and address risks in partnership with system delivery teams, escalating and resolving issues to keep projects on track while aligning with business goals.

  


**WHAT YOU’LL BRING:**



* Professional Experience: Either (1\) 3\+ years in a business role or as a Product Owner/Product Manager, with a track record of leading product development, prioritizing work, and delivering outcomes aligned to business strategy; or (2\) 5\+ years as an expert contributor on a systems team with deep knowledge of internal/external\-facing UI technology and related backend systems that support insurance activities (e.g., enrollment, premium payment, and enrollee/member data management).
* Agile Product Ownership Expertise: Ability to serve as a Product Owner for one or more Agile Release Train cars, with a conceptual understanding of the SAFe framework and its roles, processes, and collaboration methods.
* Industry and Technical Knowledge: Familiarity with the insurance industry, operational business processes, and a broad understanding of technology domains, including cloud, client\-server, SaaS, and Enterprise Architecture.
* Leadership and Communication Skills: Strong leadership, collaboration, and negotiation abilities to build relationships with stakeholders, guide teams, and resolve conflicts effectively.
* Analytical and Organizational Proficiency: Excellent time management, prioritization, and data\-driven decision\-making skills, coupled with the ability to independently manage complex, multi\-year initiatives.

  


**PREFERRED:**



* Proven ability to analyze and map complex data flows across systems, with a focus on external/internal facing User Interface (UI) and relationships to backend systems.
* Strong understanding of Group Insurance concepts around member enrollment life cycles, member management, premium and billing.
