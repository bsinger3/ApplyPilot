**Position Overview** 




 We are seeking a skilled Scrum Master to spearhead our Data Center to Cloud migration strategy. Lead cross\-functional teams through sophisticated cloud transformation projects encompassing Rehost, Re\-platform, Rewrite, and Purchase methodologies
 



  





***Key Responsibilities:***




  





**Scrum Leadership \& Facilitation** 



* Facilitate all Scrum ceremonies (Daily Stand\-ups, Sprint Planning, Sprint Reviews, and Retrospectives) for cloud migration teams
* Remove impediments and blockers that hinder team progress across multiple migration workstreams
* Coach team members on Agile principles and Scrum practices specific to cloud transformation
* Foster a culture of continuous improvement and collaboration across cross\-functional teams



  





**Cloud Migration Strategy Management** 



* **Rehost (Lift \& Shift)** 
 : Coordinate teams migrating applications with minimal changes to cloud infrastructure
* **Replatform** 
 : Manage sprints focused on optimizing applications during migration with minor modifications
* **Rewrite/Refactor** 
 : Facilitate development teams rebuilding applications using cloud\-native architectures
* **Purchase** 
 : Coordinate evaluation, procurement, and implementation of SaaS/commercial solutions replacing legacy systems



  





**Project Coordination \& Delivery** 



* Maintain and prioritize product backlogs for multiple migration streams
* Track and report on sprint velocity, burndown charts, and migration progress metrics
* Coordinate dependencies between teams working on interconnected systems
* Ensure alignment with overall cloud migration roadmap and timelines
* Manage risks and issues specific to cloud transformation initiatives



  





**Stakeholder Management** 



* Communicate progress, risks, and blockers to leadership and stakeholders
* Facilitate decision\-making sessions for migration strategy selection (6Rs framework)
* Coordinate with Infrastructure, Security, Compliance, and Application teams
* Bridge communication between technical teams and business stakeholders
