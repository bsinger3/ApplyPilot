**Role Overview**

We are seeking a **Project Manager** to lead the planning, execution, and delivery of complex digital and technology initiatives. This role is responsible for managing scope, schedule, resources, and budgets while ensuring high\-quality delivery aligned with business objectives.

The ideal candidate brings strong program leadership experience, deep expertise in Agile/SAFe environments, and the ability to manage cross\-functional teams in fast\-paced, evolving environments.

**Key Responsibilities**
* Lead end\-to\-end **project and program delivery**, including planning, execution, and continuous improvement
* Define project scope, schedules, dependencies, and budgets to ensure successful delivery outcomes
* Align solution delivery with **business goals and stakeholder expectations**
* Manage cross\-team dependencies and coordinate resources to meet timelines and scope commitments
* Engage stakeholders to capture feedback, prioritize requirements, and drive resolution of issues
* Present project status, roadmaps, and strategic updates to leadership and client stakeholders
* Drive adoption and implementation of **Scaled Agile (SAFe) methodologies** at the program level
* Lead program and release activities across **multiple Agile Release Trains (ARTs)**
* Provide **servant leadership**, governance, and execution oversight across delivery teams
* Mentor and coach Scrum Masters and team members to improve performance and Agile maturity
* Support UX alignment and ensure **customer\-centric solution delivery**
* Apply Agile and Lean principles to improve delivery efficiency and program performance
* Manage solution interfaces across internal and external systems
* Translate strategic business objectives into **execution\-ready delivery plans**
* Define success metrics and leverage data\-driven insights to guide decision\-making
* Perform functional allocation and manage task dependencies across workstreams
