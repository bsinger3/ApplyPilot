The transportation industry has no shortage of complex problems requiring creative solutions to scale efficiently. In this role, you will lead engineering teams and work closely with product to deliver best in class software to address those industry problems.

* Execute on the strategy and vision for a component of Mastery's Transportation Management System

* Build and maintain supporting documentation relating to your product(s) and feature(s)

* Prior Logistics or Supply Chain experience a bonus
