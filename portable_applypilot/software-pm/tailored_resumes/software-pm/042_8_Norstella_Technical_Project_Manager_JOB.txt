**Description**

  

  

Why MMIT? At MMIT (https://www.mmitnetwork.com/), we provide our healthcare clients with a transparent, structured view of complex drug coverage information. We are a company that values:* Collaborative teams focused on meeting goals and delivering value
* Continuous learning and opportunity to grow

  

We answer the “what” and the “why” related to drug coverage and reimbursement, leveraging technology, research, and industry experts to make sense of how and why specific pharmaceutical drugs are covered. Physicians and pharmacies use our data and applications to understand what drugs are available to patients and how they are covered by health plans. Pharmaceutical manufacturers trust our data to understand how their drug and competitive drugs are covered, which helps forecast performance and tune strategy.

  

### **Job description**

  

  

The Technical Project Manager (TPM) serves as the MMIT lead client facing technical point of contact throughout the implementation process and is responsible for presenting technical requirements, standard deployment plans and workflows, and answering technical and implementation questions. The TPM is responsible for establishing a cadence for successful internal and client communication including meetings with key stakeholders. The PM maintains and communicates the project plan/timeline with an acute understanding of the critical path, milestones, dependencies, and impact to client satisfaction. They are critical to issue resolution by assessing, escalating, and managing to mitigation.  

  

The Client Delivery Team (CDS) leads and coordinates project teams end\-to\-end for MMIT. They deliver implementation projects in scope and on\-time while adhering to effective risk management practices. This team is critical in ensuring the needs of the client and MMIT are balanced and escalated as necessary.### **Responsibilities:**

  

* Manage large projects from potentially new designs, integrations, production, and test
* Develop comprehensive project plans that merge requirements with company goals and coordinate with various managers and technical personnel during all project phases, from early adoption to GA
* Monitor project progress continuously and make detailed scheduled reports on measurable items, such as milestones and deliverables
* Proactive client communication through participation in regular scheduled meetings with sponsors and stakeholders.
* Monitors the product/program/project from initiation through delivery, interfacing with customer on all matters.
* Create client facing and internal documentation in support of post\-sale activities such as requirements documentation, Gantt charts, project plans, checklists, meeting minutes, meeting agendas, etc.
* Develops and maintains knowledge of EA products and benefits, and shares that information with CDS
* Serves as the trusted advisor and ensures project and change management follow best practices.
* Build, cultivate, and develop strategic relationships cross functionally in the organization
* Ensures consistent risk management: identification, escalation, mitigation for contingency plans/actions.
