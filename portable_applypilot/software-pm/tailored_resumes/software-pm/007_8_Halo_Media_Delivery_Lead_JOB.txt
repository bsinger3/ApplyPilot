As a
 **Delivery Lead at Halo** 
 , you’ll be the cornerstone of successful project execution, leading cross\-functional teams to deliver high\-quality software solutions. You’ll ensure that delivery processes are meticulously followed, risks are proactively mitigated, and communication with stakeholders is seamless. While you’ll collaborate closely with Client Managers on project financials where applicable, your professional development and adherence to delivery best practices will be under the direct guidance of the
 **Manager of Delivery** 
 . This role demands a proactive individual with exceptional organizational and communication skills, committed to driving continuous improvement and ensuring client satisfaction.
 


**Key Responsibilities** 
* **Project Planning \& Scope:** 
 Define clear objectives, manage a refined backlog and roadmap, and ensure scope remains within team capacity while championing innovation.
* **Execution \& Quality Assurance:** 
 Oversee daily activities and metrics to ensure consistent delivery; champion continuous improvement and proactively escalate critical blockers.
* **Team Facilitation \& Coordination:** 
 Lead all Agile ceremonies (Daily Stand\-ups, Sprints, Retros), conduct 1:1s, and maintain alignment between Tech Leads and Product Owners.
* **Financial Management:** 
 Collaborate on P\&L forecasts, meticulously track Time \& Materials (T\&M) hours, and ensure Fixed Price (FP) projects remain within budget.
* **Client Relationship Management:** 
 Act as the primary point of contact to build trust, troubleshoot issues, and ensure transparent communication through regular updates and demos.
* **Release Management:** 
 Plan and coordinate deployments with Pod Leads, ensuring thorough documentation and proactive client notification for all releases.
* **Stakeholder Satisfaction:** 
 Formally measure and document stakeholder expectations, fostering positive relationships and integrating feedback to drive alignment.


**Qualifications** 
* **At least 3 years of experience** 
 in delivery management or a similar leadership role.
* **Strong understanding** 
 of project management methodologies (e.g., Scrum, Agile).
* **Excellent communication** 
 and organizational skills.
* **Financial acumen** 
 with the ability to support tracking and ensure alignment with project budgets.
* **Proactive problem\-solving** 
 skills with the ability to prioritize tasks effectively in a fast\-paced environment.
* **Data\-driven mindset** 
 with experience using reporting tools and maintaining accurate documentation.
