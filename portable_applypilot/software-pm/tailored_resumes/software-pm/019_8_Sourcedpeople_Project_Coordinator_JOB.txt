The ideal candidate will be responsible for assisting the Project Managers (PM) in delivering
 



 e\-commerce projects on time and within budget by handling administrative tasks and coordinating resources.
 







**Responsibilities** 




  





**Project Coordination** 



* Handle administrative tasks for the PM and project team members to keep the projects running smoothly.
* Ensure the correct application of the project management process across all assigned projects.
* Create outcome led tasks from the original project discovery and scope of work that include user stories, clear descriptions and acceptance criteria.
* Assign tasks to internal resources (developers, designers, testing etc), ensuring daily monitoring and review their progress.
* Provide clear feedback on tasks to resources on open tasks, reporting any issues distinctly and defining the expected results.
* Supported by the PM, organise, attend and participate in project WIPs and stakeholder meetings, whilst documenting and following up on important decisions and actions.
* Assist the PM to maintain a detailed project plan and documentation throughout the project (project master, Jira tickets, development briefs, theme briefs, Client Success handover, external brief, external work order etc).
* Be aware of any out of scope project requests and ensure the PM raises change requests as required to prevent scope creep whilst maintaining a transparent timeline with the client.
* Submit project status reports including project milestones, deliverables, dependencies, risks and issues, communicating across leadership on an on\-going basis in the form of a report.
* Report project risks, outcomes and escalate issues to the PM according to project work plan.



  





**Team Player** 



* Be part of building a culture of diligence, success and teamwork within the Project Management team.
* Mentor new Project Coordinators to ensure alignment of responsibilities and expectations, as defined in their job description.
* Assisting with internal team communications (managing resources, assisting with blockers etc).
* Supporting, and cooperating with, all other business units to meet performance and operational targets.



  





**Qualifications** 



* Minimum 2\-3 years in a digital Project Coordinator role (or similar).
* Ideally hold a relevant project management certification, such as Certificate IV in Project Management, CAPM (PMP a plus).
