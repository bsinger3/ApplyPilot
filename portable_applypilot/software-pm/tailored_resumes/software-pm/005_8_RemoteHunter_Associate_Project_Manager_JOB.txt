 2\. About the Opportunity:
 



 The Associate Project Manager in the IT Infrastructure and Operations PMO supports leadership of complex IT programs and projects across various teams using Agile or Waterfall methodologies. This role is crucial in managing project objectives from initiation through completion, including timelines, budgets, risk mitigation, communication, and adoption. The position contributes to maintaining efficient and secure IT operations, directly impacting the organization''s ability to meet strategic goals.

 • Lead projects ensuring adherence to established standards and practices
