**About The Project Shop** 

The Project Shop (TPS) is a project management consulting firm purpose\-built for organizations that run on execution. We partner with high\-growth companies and PE\-backed businesses to embed senior\-level project leadership, build scalable PMO infrastructure, and drive the operational rigor that turns strategy into results.

We are seeking a highly organized and proactive Project Manager who thrives in fast\-paced environments, enjoys solving complex problems, and can confidently lead cross\-functional initiatives from concept through execution.

**Position Summary** 

The Project Manager will play a key role in leading and delivering strategic business and technology initiatives for clients and internal operations. This role requires strong project management expertise, exceptional communication skills, and the ability to manage multiple priorities simultaneously while fostering collaboration across diverse teams.

The ideal candidate is detail\-oriented, client\-focused, adaptable, and passionate about building scalable processes and delivering high\-quality outcomes. This individual will help drive operational excellence while supporting The Project Shop’s mission of modernizing project delivery through innovative work management practices.

***Role \& Responsibilities*** 

**Project \& Program Management** 

* Lead multiple projects simultaneously from initiation through delivery, ensuring projects are completed on time, within scope, and within budget.
* Develop and maintain detailed project plans, timelines, RAID logs, resource plans, and status reporting.
* Facilitate project kickoff meetings, sprint planning, stakeholder reviews, retrospectives, and governance sessions.
* Identify project risks, dependencies, and blockers while proactively developing mitigation strategies.
* Monitor project health, resource utilization, deliverables, and milestones across client engagements.
* Support PMO development initiatives, operational process improvements, and portfolio management activities.
* Ensure alignment between business objectives, client expectations, and project execution.

**Client \& Stakeholder Management** 

* Build strong relationships with clients, leadership teams, vendors, and project stakeholders.
* Demonstrate flexibility and responsiveness while supporting clients and teams operating across multiple time zones and varying business schedules.
* Gather business requirements and translate them into actionable project plans and workflows.
* Provide clear and professional communication regarding project progress, risks, timelines, and decisions.
* Facilitate collaboration between business users, technical teams, consultants, and external partners.
* Prepare executive\-ready presentations, reports, and project documentation.

**Process Improvement \& Operational Excellence** 

* Leverage tools such as Asana, Microsoft Teams, Jira, Smartsheet, or similar platforms to manage workflows, reporting, communication, and project execution.
* Identify opportunities to improve operational efficiency, cross\-functional collaboration, project visibility, and delivery consistency.
* Develop and maintain standardized processes, templates, documentation, governance structures, and reporting frameworks to support scalable project delivery.
* Drive continuous improvement initiatives focused on workflow optimization, resource management, and organizational effectiveness.
* Support implementation of process automation, system integrations, and operational best practices where applicable.
* Apply structured project delivery methodologies, including traditional, hybrid, and Agile\-based approaches, based on business and client needs.

**Resource \& Vendor Coordination** 

* Coordinate project resources across multiple workstreams and priorities.
* Collaborate with vendors and third\-party partners to ensure alignment with project deliverables and timelines.
* Track action items, deliverables, approvals, and dependencies to maintain momentum across initiatives.
