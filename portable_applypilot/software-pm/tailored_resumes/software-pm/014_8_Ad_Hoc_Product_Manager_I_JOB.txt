**Primary Responsibilities:**

Product Manager I (Scrum Master) \- serves as an individual contributor within a team; upon the direction and guidance of leadership, you will be responsible for supporting the goal of meeting scope, schedule and delivery requirements. Primary expectations of a Product Manager I (Scrum Master) include:


* Facilitate Agile ceremonies, including daily stand\-ups, sprint planning, backlog refinement, sprint reviews, retrospectives, and Program Increment planning activities.
* Serve as a servant leader and coach for Agile teams, helping remove impediments, promote collaboration, and encourage continuous improvement
* Partner with Product Owners, Product Managers, Release Train Engineers, and stakeholders to ensure team priorities are clearly understood and aligned to program objectives
* Help teams plan, track, and deliver committed work within each iteration and Program Increment
* Identify risks, dependencies, blockers, and delivery challenges, escalating as appropriate to ensure timely resolution
* Foster a culture of accountability, transparency, collaboration, and continuous learning within the team
* Assist teams in managing work through Agile lifecycle tools such as Jira and Confluence
* Develops effective user stories and related artifacts (e.g. business analysis, process flows, diagrams)
* Enables cross\-functional workflows and the iterative delivery of features and products
* Responsible for collecting quantitative product data
* Supports stakeholders by gathering product requirements and aligning requirements to business goals
* Help teams balance delivery commitments with sustainable pace, technical excellence, and quality standards
