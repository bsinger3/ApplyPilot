**About the Role** 




 We are looking for a
 **Project Management Officer (PMO)** 
 to plan and track the delivery of
 **projects** 
 . This role will ensure structured, phased execution across product definition, design, and technical implementation.
 



 The PMO will work
 **closely alongside the Product Analyst (PA)** 
 and cross\-functional teams to ensure clarity in scope, timelines, dependencies, and execution.
 **Experience or exposure to trading or fintech platforms is a strong advantage** 
 , especially for understanding user journeys and data\-driven features.
 



  





**Key Responsibilities** 



* Own and manage the
 **end\-to\-end project plan** 
 across product discovery, design, and delivery phases.
* Translate product roadmaps into
 **clear timelines, milestones, and phased execution plans** 
 .
* Work
 **alongside the Product Analyst** 
 to ensure requirements, user flows, and feature definitions are complete and delivery ready.
* Support the
 **creation, review, and validation of user flows** 
 .
* Coordinate with
 **product, UI/UX, content and technical teams** 
 to align scope, priorities, and execution.
* Track progress against timelines and ensure delivery aligns with
 **team capacity and priorities** 
 .
* Identify, manage, and communicate
 **dependencies, risks, and blockers** 
 , including those related to APIs, integrations, or third\-party systems.
* Ensure effective
 **scope control and change management** 
 throughout the project lifecycle.
* Facilitate
 **planning sessions, stand\-ups, reviews, and delivery checkpoints** 
 .
* Ensure all deliverables (requirements, flows, designs) are
 **reviewed and approved** 
 before development handoff.



  





**Skills \& Experience** 



* **3 \- 4 years of experience** 
 as a PMO or Project Manager.
* Experience working closely with
 **Product Analysts, Product Owners, or Business Analysts** 
 .
* Exposure to
 **user flow creation, journey mapping, or functional flow documentation** 
 (supporting role).
* Strong capability in managing
 **phased deliveries** 
 and complex dependencies.
* Familiarity with
 **API\-driven systems and third\-party integrations** 
 .
* Proficiency with tools such as
 **Jira, Confluence, ClickUp/Trello, Figma (for flow reviews), and Google Workspace** 
 .
* Good communication in English.
