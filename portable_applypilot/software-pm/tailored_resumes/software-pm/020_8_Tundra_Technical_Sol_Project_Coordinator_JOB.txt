**Job Title:** 
 Project Coordinator
 



**Location:** 
 Chicago Preferred
 



**Contract Duration:** 
 6 months to possible extension
 



**Job Overview:** 
 We are seeking a highly motivated and organized Project Coordinator to work closely with the Program Manager, supporting the delivery of critical components within a large\-scale technology program. This role is ideal for someone early in their project management career who has strong attention to detail, organizational skills, and a “can\-do” attitude.
 



**Key Responsibilities:** 



* Support Program Manager in managing deliverables and release schedules across multiple technology workstreams.
* Track and chase critical program/project documents and ensure timely completion and filing.
* Assist in release management, coordinating schedules, dependencies, and communications between teams.
* Serve as a point of contact for documentation, reporting, and operational support.
* Help plan, monitor, and report on project milestones, deliverables, and key metrics.
* Provide general project/program management support as assigned.



**Qualifications:** 



* 1\-3 years’ experience in project coordination/management, preferably in technology, banking, or software environments.
* Demonstrated experience supporting the delivery of technology initiatives.
* Strong organizational and time management skills, with the ability to multitask and prioritize.
* Comfort with document management, tracking, and status reporting.
* Effective communicator with stakeholders at different levels.
* Self\-starter, able to work independently and as part of a team.
* Experience with project management tools (e.g., MS Project, Jira, or similar) is a plus.
