**About the Opportunity**



GAMA\-1 Technologies, LLC is seeking an experienced IT Business Analyst III to support Agile delivery across critical programs and serve as the Scrum Master in a **remote** work environment. In this role, you will serve as a servant leader and facilitator, enabling high\-performing teams, removing impediments, and fostering a culture of continuous improvement. The IT Business Analyst III plays a key role in aligning business priorities with technical execution by promoting collaboration among stakeholders, product owners, and development teams. Success in this role is measured by improved team performance, transparent delivery practices, and the consistent delivery of high\-quality solutions that support mission and organizational goals.

**What You Will Do in This Role**



The IT Business Analyst III will serve as a Scrum Master and Agile delivery facilitator, leading cross\-functional teams across project delivery and operational support functions while ensuring alignment between business needs and technical execution. It combines Agile leadership, business analysis, and service operations coordination to support delivery, process improvement, and operational readiness.

  



Responsibilities include:


* Lead Agile delivery as Scrum Master, facilitating ceremonies, removing impediments, and enabling high\-performing teams across delivery and operational support functions
* Collaborate with product owners, stakeholders, and technical teams, including service delivery and support teams, to ensure alignment between business needs and delivery
* Gather, analyze, and document business and functional requirements, including service desk functions and operational procedures, to support solution development
* Translate business needs into user stories and technical requirements, supporting backlog refinement and prioritization for both project delivery and service operations enhancements
* Drive process improvement and recommend enhancements to systems, workflows, and service desk operational processes
* Support testing activities, including user acceptance testing, to ensure solutions meet business requirements and operational readiness
* Maintain clear documentation and ensure transparency across teams and stakeholders, including operational reporting and knowledge documentation
* Partner with leadership and PMO to support planning, coordination, and successful delivery across multiple teams and operational support functions

  


**What You Will Bring**


* Bachelor’s degree in computer science, information technology, or a related field
* 4 \- 8 years of experience as a Scrum Master, championing Agile values, principles, and practices within the team and program while fostering a culture of continuous improvement
* Relevant Agile or business analysis certifications such as Scrum Master, Product Owner, CBAP, or PMI PBA
* Experience facilitating Scrum ceremonies, including sprint planning, daily stand\-ups, sprint reviews, and retrospectives to ensure team alignment and efficiency
* 4 \- 8 years of experience applying software development life cycle methodologies, including Agile and Waterfall, to support effective delivery
