Hanson Consulting Group is seeking a skilled
 **Technical Program Manager** 
 to support our client's team in delivering complex software initiatives across enterprise applications.
 



  





 In this role, you will be responsible for driving end\-to\-end feature delivery for application updates that support business\-critical workflows and customer\-facing experiences.
 



  





 You will work closely with technical program managers, engineering teams, and business stakeholders to define requirements, design solutions, manage delivery timelines, and communicate progress across multiple workstreams.
 



  





**Responsibilities:** 




  




* Write requirements and functional specifications for new and updated features
* Support sprint planning and release planning for application updates
* Participate in engineering standups and delivery coordination
* Ensure systems meet security, compliance, and organizational standards
* Partner with engineering and stakeholders to define and execute functional and user acceptance testing (UAT), including scope definition, test planning, coordination, triage, and status reporting
* Support the full product lifecycle, including definition, planning, release, maintenance, and feature deprecation
* Produce weekly status reports and communicate in\-flight work and delivery progress



  





**Ideal Candidate Experience:** 




  




* 6 \+ years of experience in technical program or project management
* Strong written and verbal communication skills
* Familiarity with software development lifecycle and delivery processes
* Experience working with compliance and security requirements
* Experience with Agile delivery tools (e.g., Azure DevOps or similar platforms)
* Ability to proactively identify risks and coordinate cross\-team resolutions
* Experience working with multiple stakeholders across engineering and business teams
* Must have a strong understanding of both program and project management principles
