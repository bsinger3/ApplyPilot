**About Orive.AI**

Orive is a performance\-driven marketing agency helping brands grow through paid media, creative, websites, reporting, and operational systems.

We are seeking an AI\-forward operator to help improve the systems, workflows, and operational infrastructure that support both client delivery and internal execution.

This role sits at the intersection of marketing operations, websites, reporting, automation, and AI\-assisted execution.

This is not a traditional software engineering role; however, candidates should have a strong understanding of how digital systems connect and should be comfortable using tools such as **Codex, Claude Code, ChatGPT, Cursor, and other AI\-assisted workflows** to improve processes, troubleshoot issues, and support execution across projects.

Candidates may come from backgrounds such as:

* Website project management
* Marketing operations
* Agency operations
* Growth operations
* Technical account management
* SaaS implementation or onboarding
* No\-code automation
* Web or marketing production

The ideal candidate is organized, proactive, detail\-oriented, and comfortable operating in a fast\-moving environment with evolving tools and workflows.
