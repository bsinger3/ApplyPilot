 **Position Summary:**


The Implementation Specialist is responsible for delivering a seamless, high\-quality onboarding experience for new PatientNow customers. This role owns the critical first phase of the customer journey, ensuring each client is successfully configured, trained, and prepared to adopt the platform with confidence.


This position combines project management, software configuration, and customer training. You will guide customers through change, help them transition from legacy workflows, and ensure they realize value quickly. Success in this role means delivering efficient, well\-organized implementations while creating a strong first impression that builds long\-term trust.

 **Essential Duties and Responsibilities:**

* Own the end\-to\-end onboarding experience for new customers, from kickoff through successful go\-live
* Configure and customize the PatientNow platform to align with each customer’s workflows and business needs
* Manage onboarding timelines, milestones, and deliverables to ensure efficient and on\-time implementations
* Deliver clear, engaging training sessions tailored to different user roles, learning styles, and levels of technical proficiency
* Guide customers through change management, helping them adopt new processes and workflows with confidence
* Identify risks to onboarding success early and proactively drive solutions
* Provide consultative recommendations to help customers optimize their use of the platform
* Communicate technical concepts in a user\-friendly, professional manner
* Maintain accurate documentation of onboarding progress, configurations, and customer interactions
* Stay current on new features, product updates, and industry trends to continuously improve onboarding outcomes
