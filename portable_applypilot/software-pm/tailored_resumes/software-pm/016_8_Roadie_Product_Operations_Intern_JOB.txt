As a **Product Operations Intern** at Roadie, you’ll support the Product team by helping maintain project documentation, organize Jira workflows, and assist with reporting and coordination efforts. This internship is a great opportunity for someone interested in project management, operations, or product development to gain hands\-on experience working in a collaborative, fast\-paced environment.


#### **What You’ll Do**


* Help review and organize outdated or redundant project items within Jira and related tools
* Support documentation efforts by updating process guides, templates, and team resources
* Collaborate with team members to identify opportunities for improving project organization and workflow consistency
* Assist with meeting coordination, note\-taking, action item tracking, and follow\-up communication
* Help prepare project summaries, status updates, and internal documentation
* Participate in PM and Scrum team meetings to gain exposure to Agile project management practices
* Assist with creating and maintaining Jira dashboards, reports, and tracking systems to help support project visibility and organization
* Support the Project Manager with day\-to\-day operational and coordination tasks

#### **What You Bring**


* Strong organizational skills and attention to detail
* Interest in project management, operations, or product development
* Ability to manage multiple tasks and communicate effectively with team members
* Some experience with project management systems or comfortable learning new tools in a fast\-paced environment
* Experience coordinating school projects, student organizations, events, or other collaborative activities is a plus
* Familiarity with Jira, Google Workspace, or similar collaboration tools is helpful but not required
* Exposure to Agile or Scrum concepts through coursework, internships, or personal projects is a plus
* Positive attitude, willingness to learn, and eagerness to contribute to a collaborative team environment


**What You'll Get**


* Exposure to multiple development teams and processes in an Agile environment
* Develop practical project management skills, gain exposure to industry\-standard tools, and contribute directly to process improvements
* Hands\-on experience with reporting, which will be utilized by our software development teams
* Mentorship with Project Manager/Scrum Master
