 At Ciena, this role drives the successful planning and launch of new products that support our customers’ most demanding connectivity needs. Serving as a central leader across engineering, product, and customer‑facing teams, the Project Manager ensures that every product release progresses with clarity, discipline, and a focus on delivering high‑quality outcomes.
   

  

**How you will make an impact:**
* Lead cross‑functional teams through Ciena’s new product introduction and lifecycle management processes
* Coordinate release plans that deliver committed scope on schedule and with high quality
* Mitigate risk by developing and executing comprehensive risk‑management plans
* Manage project schedules, scope, quality, and cost, ensuring transparent reporting to stakeholders
* Identify performance issues and guide the team through recovery planning and execution
* Maintain centralized project documentation and ensure data accuracy in project‑status systems
* Support early customer engagements by coordinating deliverables and resolving pre‑release issues

* Bachelor’s degree, preferably in computer science, engineering, or a related field (\+)

* Proven success leading complex software and hardware project deliveries (\+)
* Strong written and verbal communication skills, including influence and negotiation (\+)
* Solid project‑management capabilities across planning, risk assessment, issue resolution, and performance tracking (\+)
* Ability to motivate and guide cross‑functional teams toward shared goals (\+)
* High attention to detail with strong organizational and problem‑solving skills (\+)


**Nice To Haves**
* Experience in product‑development environments
* PMP certification
* Demonstrated skill in managing change, ambiguity, and evolving priorities
* Ability to quickly learn technical concepts related to network products
* Experience working in fast‑paced, highly collaborative technology organizations
