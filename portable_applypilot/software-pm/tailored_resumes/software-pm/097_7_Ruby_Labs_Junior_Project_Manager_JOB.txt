**About The Role**
 We're looking for a detail\-obsessed Junior PM who will own task breakdown and backlog management, transforming high\-level requirements into crystal\-clear, production\-ready tasks that keep our team moving fast.
   

  

 You'll be the clarity engine \- ensuring every task is complete, unambiguous, and ready for execution without back\-and\-forth questions.
   

  

**We move fast.** 
 Expect flexible working hours during critical phases. This isn't a 9\-to\-5 role, it's for people who are genuinely invested in shipping great products.
   

  

****Key Responsibilities****
* Own task breakdown: Take feature requirements and break them into clear, actionable tasks that developers, data analysts, or other teams can execute without confusion.
* Manage the backlog: Maintain a 7\-day forward\-looking backlog at all times, ensuring the team always knows what's next.
* Ensure task quality: Every task you create must include Why (business reason), What (exact requirements), Acceptance Criteria (how we know it's done), Definition of Done (technical checklist), and corner cases covered.
* Work with the Project Owner: Translate high\-level product strategy into detailed execution plans.
* Keep everything tracked: 100% of tasks live in our task management system \- zero work happens outside the system.
* Achieve 90%\+ quality rate: Your tasks should rarely need revisions because they're complete and clear from the start.


**Required Experience**
* 1–2 years in project coordination, product operations, or task management roles.
* Hands\-on experience with task management tools (Jira, Asana, ClickUp, Linear, or similar).
* Experience working with AI tools or AI\-related product features.
* Proven ability to write clear, detailed documentation or task descriptions.
* Track record of working in structured, process\-driven environments.

****Nice to have****
