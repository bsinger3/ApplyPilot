**Role Description** 




 The IT TGO Solutions Project Manager will be responsible for leading cross\-functional initiatives that support the implementation and optimization of advanced Supply Chain planning systems and related tools \& technologies. The role ensures successful project delivery by coordinating stakeholders across IT and business functions within a matrix organization. This position drives project execution, manages timelines, risks, and dependencies, and ensures clear and effective communication with key stakeholders. The Project Manager will also support system integrations and data flows between enterprise platforms, aligning business requirements with technical solutions to enable efficient processes and the successful adoption of advanced analytics technologies in a fast\-paced environment.

* Lead cross\-functional projects in a fast\-paced environment, ensuring delivery within tight timelines and competing priorities

* Facilitate alignment between IT and business teams to ensure smooth implementation of systems and process improvements
