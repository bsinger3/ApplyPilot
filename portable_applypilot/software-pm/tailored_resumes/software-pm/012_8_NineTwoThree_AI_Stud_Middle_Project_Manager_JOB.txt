**What You'll Do**
* Drive 2\-3 projects in the active stage from start to finish and deliver value to our customers
* Define project requirements and deliverables, plan and manage their execution, monitoring and adjustments
* Interpretation of business requirements to the technical team and vice versa. Estimating development efforts
* Participating in customer calls and providing a project update to the customer
* Organizing and keeping up\-to\-date Project boards. Documenting tasks: timeline, user stories and developer's tasks incl. detailed Acceptance Criteria
* Coordinating the development teams including QA and designers
* Testing the system before the releases
* Taking full responsibility and ownership for project delivery. Monitor project progress, identify potential risks or bottlenecks, and proactively address them
* Helping with onboarding newcomers and mentoring Junior PMs
