* Job Overview
* Enforces organizational policies and establishes goals for project teams which develop business applications and information services products.


**Responsibilities**
* Manages, and controls scope, schedule and budget for multiple cross functional projects.
* Works with management across the organization to define, plan, develop and implement new information products and services.
* Leads cross\-functional teams in the execution of full product development life cycle development. Initiates, plans, executes, controls and closes software and product development activity.
* Under general direction, responsible for all aspects of the development and implementation of assigned projects and provides a single point of contact for those projects.
* Interfaces with all areas affected by the project including clients, computer services, and client services.
* Plans and manages resource allocations and task assignments within a matrix managed environment.
* Communicates project status and risks, with associated recommendations, to all stakeholders.
* Ensures adherence to quality standards and reviews project deliverables.
* Provides business guidance to project team.
* Leverages approved AI‑assisted tools to support project planning, forecasting, risk identification, risk trend analysis and scenario modeling, RAID management and reporting for different audiences.
* Applies AI‑supported analysis to project financials, capacity planning, and dependency modeling to improve delivery predictability and decision‑making.


**Minimum Required Education And Experience**
* Bachelor's Degree


**Required Knowledge, Skills And Abilities**
* Five (5\) years of experience in software and/or product development
* Project management education and experience.
* Past delivery experience in COTS products / configuration projects; and Gated, hybrid, and agile development environments
* Organizations with regulatory compliance requirements
* Having a working knowledge of SDLC, regulated environments, business acumen, financial management.
* Healthcare /life sciences environment domain knowledge is a plus
* High level experience with delivery tools such as: Requirements, DevOps, Testing, Defect Management tools (HP ALM, JIRA, ADO, etc.)
* Financial Management Systems (e.g. Oracle, Peoplesoft, homegrown, etc.) is a plus
* Project Management Organization (PMO) Center of Excellence (COE)
* Lean Six Sigma and waste elimination methodologies
* Project Financials
* Project management processes (RAID, Portfolio Management, Intake,  QCD Analysis, etc. )
* IT Maturity Process Improvement mindset
* KPI and Metrics Managed organizations
* Project Delivery Culture with quality, timeliness, within budget, and optimization of resource utilization
