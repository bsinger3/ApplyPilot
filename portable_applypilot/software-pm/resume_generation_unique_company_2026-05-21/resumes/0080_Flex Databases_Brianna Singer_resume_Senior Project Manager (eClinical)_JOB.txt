Our clients include biotech and pharmaceutical companies, as well as Contract Research Organizations (CROs) that outsource clinical trial management.  If you have a deep background in eClinical solutions and want to transition from being a cog in the machine to a key driver of product and process, we want to talk to you.  You will lead complex system implementations and manage project timelines, budgets, and resources effectively.  Responsibilities: - Lead project planning and execution for eClinical solutions. - Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure project success. - Communicate project status to stakeholders and manage expectations. - Identify and mitigate project risks. - Ensure compliance with industry regulations and standards.  Requirements: - Proven experience as a Project Manager in the eClinical or pharmaceutical industry. - Strong understanding of clinical trial processes and eClinical technologies. - Excellent communication and leadership skills. - Ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously. - Relevant certifications (PMP, PRINCE2) are a plus.
