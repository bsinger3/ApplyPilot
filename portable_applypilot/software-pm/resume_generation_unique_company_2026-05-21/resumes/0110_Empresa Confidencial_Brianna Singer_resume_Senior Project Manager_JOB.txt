About the Role
We are looking for a Project Specialist with strong experience leading large-scale, multi-country programs, ideally within the banking or financial services sector.
This role requires a professional capable of managing multiple initiatives simultaneously, aligning stakeholders across regions, ensuring governance, controlling risks and costs, and driving execution in complex environments. You will work closely with technical teams, business leaders, and international stakeholders to deliver high-impact programs.
Key Responsibilities
- Lead and coordinate multiple projects within a program, ensuring alignment with strategic objectives
- Manage scope, timeline, budget, risks, dependencies, and resources at program level
- Establish and maintain program governance, reporting, and escalation mechanisms
- Work closely with regional and international stakeholders, including business, technology, and vendors
- Ensure delivery using Agile, Hybrid, or Waterfall methodologies as required
- Identify risks and proactively propose mitigation strategies
- Track KPIs, milestones, and financial performance of the program
- Communicate progress, risks, and outcomes clearly to leadership
Required Qualifications
- 3+ years of experience as a Program Manager or Senior Project Manager
- Proven experience managing large, complex programs (multiple projects, teams, or countries)
- Background in banking, financial services, or regulated environments
