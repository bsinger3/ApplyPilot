 This role calls for sharp judgment, comfort with ambiguity, and the confidence to influence decisions across stakeholders without leaning on a title.  You know what it takes to move a project from kickoff to impact—thoughtful resource planning, on\-time delivery, proactive risk mitigation, and clear communication on status and progress. You'll partner with consultants across disciplines to deliver work that consistently exceeds client expectations. **Primary Responsibilities**
**Project Delivery \& Execution**
* Build strong relationships with clients to support measurable and impactful working relationships.
* Serve as a key point of contact for project stakeholders, supporting the consulting team in communications with the client.
* Build strong relationships with clients to support measurable and impactful working relationships
* Ensure on\-time, on\-budget, and in\-scope delivery of defined projects that deliver a high degree of client value and satisfaction.
* Understand each client's business, goals and priorities, translating strategic plans into actionable project plans, tasks and timelines for the delivery team.
* Work with consulting teams to ensure successful deliverables and outcomes are attained and that project value and impact are effectively tracked. Examples include Account Plans, scoped deliverables and client stories.
* Assist in the coordination of high\-impact meetings, developing detailed agendas and tracking action items and next steps.
* Facilitate effective meetings focused on outcomes. Participate in discussions and ensure action items are identified, tracked and completed.
* Partner with consultant teams to identify and present new client growth opportunities through the sales cycle.
* Responsible for risk mitigation on all assigned projects: Identified risks and mitigation strategies and escalate as appropriate.


**Resource Coordination**
* Act as the central traffic and resource coordinator for assigned clients, managing workflows, project plans and team assignments to ensure all commitments are delivered by their assigned dates.
* Develop and maintain accurate resource projections and capacity plans, proactively identifying the need for an increase of decrease in assigned resources to maintain delivery excellence.
* Effectively lead project ceremonies, including resource planning, prioritization, stand\-ups and retrospectives and deliverable reviews to ensure quality and timeliness.
* Manage and prioritize any project backlog in collaboration with consulting leads to ensure work aligns with client objectives and team capacity.
* Identify, track, communicate and mitigate project risks, particularly those related to resource constraints, dependencies, timelines and cost overruns.


**Process \& Operations**
* Help ensure the highest level of quality for all project deliverables by coordinating and managing quality assurance checks and review cycles.
* Responsible for creating and maintaining clear project documentation, including project plans, resource forecasts, issue and task logs and status reports.
