We are seeking a highly skilled System Analyst / Technical Product Manager to lead and support the end\-to\-end migration from Microsoft Dynamics Great Plains (GP) to Oracle ERP. This role requires a blend of functional expertise, technical acumen, and product management capabilities to ensure a smooth transition, optimization of business processes, and successful adoption of Oracle ERP solutions.

 **Key Responsibilities:**


ERP Transformation \& Migration:

* Lead the migration strategy and execution from Microsoft Dynamics Great Plains (GP) to Oracle ERP (e.g., Oracle Fusion Cloud / Oracle EBS).
* Conduct current\-state (AS\-IS) assessments of GP systems and design future\-state (TO\-BE) Oracle ERP solutions.
* Define data migration strategy, including data mapping, cleansing, validation, and reconciliation.
* Collaborate with cross\-functional teams to ensure seamless integration with upstream and downstream systems.


Business Analysis \& Process Optimization:

* Gather, analyze, and document business requirements across Finance, Procurement, Supply Chain, and related domains.
* Translate business needs into functional and technical specifications.
* Identify process improvements and recommend best practices aligned with Oracle ERP capabilities.
* Facilitate workshops, gap analysis, and solution design sessions.


Technical Product Management:

* Own product backlog for ERP transformation initiatives and prioritize features based on business value.
* Define product roadmap and milestones for ERP implementation and enhancements.
* Work closely with engineering, vendors, and system integrators to deliver scalable solutions.
* Ensure alignment between business stakeholders and technical teams.


System Integration \& Architecture:

* Design integration approaches between Oracle ERP and external systems (e.g., HR, CRM, data warehouses).
* Collaborate with technical architects on API design, middleware, and data flows.
* Ensure system scalability, performance, and security compliance.


Testing \& Deployment:

* Oversee test planning and execution (SIT, UAT, regression testing).
* Validate migrated data and ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Support go\-live planning, cutover activities, and post\-implementation stabilization.


Stakeholder Management:

* Act as a bridge between business users, IT, and external vendors.
* Provide regular status updates, risks, and mitigation strategies to leadership.
* Manage expectations and drive consensus across diverse stakeholders.


Change Management \& Training:

* Support organizational change management initiatives.
* Develop training materials and conduct end\-user training sessions.
* Ensure user adoption and continuous improvement post\-implementation.

 **Required Qualifications**:

* 5\-10\+ years of experience in ERP systems, with hands\-on experience in:
* Microsoft Dynamics GP (Great Plains)
* Oracle ERP (Fusion or EBS)
* Proven experience in ERP migration or transformation projects.
* Strong understanding of financial systems (GL, AP, AR, Fixed Assets, etc.).
* Experience in data migration tools, SQL, and integration technologies.

 **Preferred Qualifications**:

* Experience with Oracle Cloud (Fusion) modules.
* Certifications in Oracle ERP, PMP, or Agile/Scrum.
* Familiarity with middleware platforms (e.g., Oracle Integration Cloud, MuleSoft).
* Knowledge of reporting tools (BI, Oracle Analytics, Power BI).
