Senior Technical Project Manager (Remote | Philippines)
About magentIQ
magentIQ is a leading technology company focused on delivering innovative solutions to our clients. We are looking for a Senior Technical Project Manager to join our team in the Philippines. This is a remote position.

- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define project scope, goals, and deliverables.
- Develop project plans, timelines, and budgets.
- Monitor project progress and make adjustments as necessary.

- 5+ years of experience in project management, preferably in a technical environment.
- Strong understanding of project management methodologies (Agile, Scrum, etc.).
- Excellent communication and leadership skills.
- Ability to work independently and manage multiple priorities.
- Experience with project management tools (e.g., JIRA, Trello).
If you are passionate about technology and project management, we would love to hear from you!
