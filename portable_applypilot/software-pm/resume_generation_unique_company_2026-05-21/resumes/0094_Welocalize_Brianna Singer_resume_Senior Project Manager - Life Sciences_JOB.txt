About Welo Life Sciences  Welo Life Sciences, a Welo Global brand, is a specialized language and technology partner serving pharmaceutical, CRO, and medical device organizations operating in regulated global markets. We deliver regulator-ready translation, linguistic validation, and localization solutions that support clinical trials, pharmacovigilance, regulatory affairs, labeling, and global commercialization. Our approach combines subject matter expertise with ISO-certified quality processes and technology-enabled workflows designed to integrate seamlessly into life sciences operations. With extensive experience supporting global product development and post-market requirements, Welo Life Sciences helps organizations accelerate timelines, protect data integrity, and maintain compliance across languages and regions. All solutions are delivered within a secure, audit-ready framework supported by seven ISO certifications.  Role Overview  We are looking for an experienced and collaborative Senior Project Manager to join our team and help drive the successful delivery of complex, global projects for our clients. In this role, you will manage key customer accounts end-to-end — from project planning and execution through to delivery and closure — using Welo Life Sciences's project management standards and tools. You will also partner closely with the Operations Squad Lead to support large enterprise accounts and strategic initiatives. As a Senior Project Manager, you will act as a trusted partner to both clients and internal teams, ensuring projects are delivered on time, within budget, and to the highest quality standards. You will collaborate cross-functionally with account directors, production teams, vendors, and stakeholders around the world to create exceptional customer experiences and drive operational excellence. The ideal candidate is proactive, organized, and solutions-oriented, with strong relationship-building skills and the ability to thrive in a fast-paced, international environment.
