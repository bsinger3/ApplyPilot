Implementation Project Manager
About Dayforce
Dayforce is a leading provider of cloud-based human capital management (HCM) software. We are committed to delivering innovative solutions that empower organizations to manage their workforce effectively.
Responsibilities:
- Lead the implementation of Dayforce solutions for clients in Australia and New Zealand.
- Collaborate with clients to understand their business needs and configure the software accordingly.
- Provide training and support to clients during the implementation process.
- Ensure project milestones are met and deliverables are completed on time.
- Act as the primary point of contact for clients throughout the implementation.
Requirements:
- Proven experience in project management, preferably in software implementation.
- Strong understanding of HCM software and its applications.
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
- Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
- Relevant certifications in project management are a plus.
