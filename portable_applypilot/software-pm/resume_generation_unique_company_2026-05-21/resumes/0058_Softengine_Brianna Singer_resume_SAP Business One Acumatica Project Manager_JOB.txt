The ideal candidate combines strong project management discipline with ERP implementation expertise, executive\-level communication skills, and the ability to drive alignment across clients, consultants, developers, and leadership teams.


This position plays a critical role in ensuring projects are delivered on time, within budget, and with high customer satisfaction while supporting long\-term client retention and growth.
