ABOUT THE ENGAGEMENT:
The Program Manager is the single LTTS face to Client and the broader Client CX leadership team. This role is the linchpin of the entire engagement — responsible for client trust, delivery integrity, commercial health, and overall program success. The Program Manager will lead a team of professionals to ensure that the project is delivered on time, within scope, and within budget. This role requires strong leadership, communication, and organizational skills to manage multiple stakeholders and drive project outcomes.
RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Act as the primary point of contact for the client and internal teams.
- Develop and maintain project plans, schedules, and budgets.
- Ensure project deliverables meet quality standards and client expectations.
- Manage risks and issues proactively to minimize impact on project delivery.
- Facilitate communication between stakeholders and project teams.
- Conduct regular project status meetings and provide updates to stakeholders.
REQUIREMENTS:
- Bachelor’s degree in a relevant field; Master’s degree preferred.
- 5+ years of experience in project management, preferably in a technology or consulting environment.
- Strong understanding of project management methodologies and tools.
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
- Ability to work in a fast-paced environment and manage multiple priorities.
- PMP or equivalent certification is a plus.
