**About this Role:**  

  

This role will be part of the Strategy \& Advisory Services group within the Enterprise Transformation division at Slipstream LS. Our strategic advisory services help life sciences organizations navigate complexity, make confident decisions, and build operating models designed to scale in regulated environments.  

  

Enterprise transformation often begins with technology decisions that carry long\-term consequences. Without a clear strategy, organizations risk platform sprawl, rework, and misalignment across commercial, medical, and R\&D teams. Our Strategy \& Advisory services address this challenge by helping organizations define a clear enterprise direction before systems are selected or expanded. The focus is on aligning platforms, processes, and operating models to support both current needs and future growth. Strategic advisory services that help life sciences organizations navigate complexity, make confident decisions, and build operating models designed to scale in regulated environments.  

  

The Program Manager role will support our CRM Implementation and Migration services for some of our most important clients. When CRM implementations are rushed, fragmented, or poorly aligned to operating models, organizations face adoption challenges, compliance risk, and costly rework. The Program Manager role will deliver structured, life sciences–ready CRM platforms designed to scale. The focus will be on harmonizing data, processes, and profiles while ensuring compliance and usability from day one. This role will be responsible for end\-to\-end CRM platform migrations and implementations for some of our clients’ most challenging eco\-systems, which include managing multiple workstreams simultaneously.  

  

**Responsibilities of Role:**


* Lead end\-to\-end CRM migration programs for life sciences clients, including assessment, planning, execution, and post\-migration support across Salesforce, Veeva CRM, and other enterprise CRM platforms.
* Develop comprehensive program plans, timelines, resource allocations, and risk mitigation strategies for complex CRM migration initiatives involving multiple stakeholders and business units.
* Coordinate cross\-functional teams including technical architects, business analysts, developers, quality assurance, change management, third\-party vendors and client stakeholders to ensure successful program delivery.
* Manage program scope, budget, schedule, and quality metrics while maintaining alignment with client business objectives and regulatory requirements (GxP, 21 CFR Part 11\).
* Conduct current\-state assessments of existing CRM environments, documenting business processes, data structures, integrations, customizations, and identifying migration risks and dependencies.
* Define data migration strategies including data mapping, cleansing, validation, and cutover planning to ensure data integrity throughout the migration process
* Partner with business owners and IT system owners to establish requirements, perform gap analysis, and design future\-state CRM solutions optimized for life sciences commercial operations.
* Oversee configuration and customization activities during migration including workflows, validation rules, page layouts, reports, dashboards, and integrations with enterprise systems (ERP, data warehouses, marketing automation).
* Manage vendor relationships and coordinate with Salesforce, Veeva, and third\-party integration partners to resolve technical issues, and ensure platform readiness
* Lead change management activities including stakeholder communications, training program development, user adoption strategies, and post\-go\-live support planning.
* Facilitate program governance meetings with executive stakeholders, providing status updates, escalating risks and issues, and securing decision\-making on critical program milestones.
* Ensure all migration activities comply with quality management system requirements, maintaining documentation in adherence with change control processes and validation protocols.
* Manage program financials including budget forecasting, invoice tracking, resource utilization, and variance reporting to maintain program profitability.
* Identify opportunities for process improvement and CRM optimization post\-migration, developing roadmaps for ongoing platform enhancements and business value realization.
* Mentor and develop junior program managers and project coordinators, fostering knowledge sharing and professional growth within the CRM practice.
