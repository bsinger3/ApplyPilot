**Position Summary**  

We are seeking a Project Manager with a strong technical foundation in Azure infrastructure to lead the planning, coordination, and execution of complex cloud deployment initiatives. This role is responsible for managing cross\-functional project teams delivering Azure\-based infrastructure solutions, including landing zones, networking, identity, security, governance, migration, and platform services. The ideal candidate combines disciplined project delivery leadership with enough technical depth to engage architects, engineers, security teams, and customer stakeholders effectively.


This individual will serve as the central point of coordination across business, technical, and operational workstreams to ensure Azure infrastructure projects are delivered on time, within scope, and aligned to security, compliance, and operational readiness requirements.


**Key Responsibilities**
========================

* Lead end\-to\-end delivery of Azure infrastructure deployment projects, from initiation through planning, execution, testing, cutover, and transition to operations.
* Develop and maintain integrated project plans, schedules, milestones, RAID logs, dependency tracking, and executive status reporting.
* Coordinate cross\-functional teams including cloud architects, Azure engineers, network engineers, identity engineers, security teams, operations teams, and third\-party vendors.
* Drive deployment planning for core Azure infrastructure components such as landing zones, subscriptions, management groups, virtual networks, connectivity, identity integration, monitoring, backup, and disaster recovery.
* Translate technical delivery activities into clear project plans, timelines, work packages, and stakeholder communications.
* Manage project resources to standards.
* Facilitate project\-related meetings, such as design reviews, readiness reviews, risk reviews, and management updates.
* Identify delivery risks, environmental constraints, and interdependencies early, and drive mitigation plans to resolution.
* Ensure alignment with enterprise security, compliance, and operational standards, especially for regulated or mission\-critical environments.
* Partner with technical leads to validate deployment readiness, testing completion, cutover planning, and support transition requirements.
* Track deliverables, action items, and decisions to maintain execution discipline and accountability across teams.
* Support customer and leadership communications with concise, accurate reporting on progress, blockers, financials, and next steps.
