**Job Summary** 




 We are seeking an experienced and servant\-leader\-oriented Scrum Master to facilitate Agile practices and support high\-performing development teams. In this role, you will guide teams in adopting Scrum principles, remove impediments, and foster a culture of continuous improvement to deliver high\-quality products efficiently.
 



  





**Key Responsibilities** 



* Facilitate Scrum ceremonies (daily stand\-ups, sprint planning, sprint reviews, retrospectives)
* Coach team members on Agile principles, Scrum framework, and best practices
* Identify and remove impediments that may hinder team progress
* Promote collaboration and effective communication within the team and with stakeholders
* Track and report on team progress using Agile metrics (velocity, burndown charts)
* Support Product Owners in backlog grooming and prioritization
* Foster a culture of continuous improvement and accountability
* Ensure adherence to Agile/Scrum practices and organizational standards
* Assist in scaling Agile practices across multiple teams if applicable



  





**Qualifications** 



* Bachelor’s degree in Business, Computer Science, or a related field
* Strong understanding of Agile methodologies and Scrum framework
* Experience facilitating Agile ceremonies and coaching teams
* Excellent communication, leadership, and conflict resolution skills
* Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast\-paced environment



  





**Preferred Skills** 



* Scrum Master certification (e.g., CSM, PSM)
* Experience with Agile tools (e.g., Jira, Confluence, Azure DevOps)
* Familiarity with software development lifecycle (SDLC)
* Experience working with cross\-functional or distributed teams
* Knowledge of scaling frameworks (e.g., SAFe, LeSS)
