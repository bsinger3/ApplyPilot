We are on the lookout for a highly organized Project Coordinator / Scrum Master to support delivery execution across multiple teams. This role is ideal for someone who can drive coordination, track progress, and ensure smooth Agile ceremonies, while also acting as a bridge between stakeholders, product teams, and leadership.
   

  

 The ideal candidate is execution\-focused, detail\-oriented, and comfortable working in fast\-paced, cross\-functional environments, with the ability to balance coordination responsibilities and Agile facilitation.
   

  

**Key Responsibilities**
* Coordinate and track day\-to\-day project activities, timelines, and deliverables.
* Facilitate Agile ceremonies (daily stand\-ups, sprint planning, retrospectives, backlog grooming).
* Support multiple teams or workstreams, ensuring alignment and progress tracking.
* Maintain and update project plans, status reports, and dashboards (Jira/Confluence).
* Monitor sprint progress, velocity, and delivery metrics.
* Identify risks, blockers, and dependencies, and escalate when needed.
* Work closely with Product Owners, Engineering teams, and stakeholders to ensure clarity on priorities.
* Assist in resource coordination and scheduling.
* Ensure documentation, meeting notes, and action items are captured and followed up on.
* Drive continuous improvement initiatives across teams.


**Required Skills \& Experience**
* 3 7\+ years of experience as a Project Coordinator, Scrum Master, or similar role.
* Strong understanding of Agile/Scrum frameworks and delivery lifecycle.
* Hands\-on experience with Jira, Confluence, and Agile reporting dashboards.
* Experience supporting multiple teams or cross\-functional programs.
* Strong organizational, multitasking, and coordination skills.
* Ability to manage stakeholder communication and expectations.
* Experience tracking KPIs such as velocity, burndown, cycle time, and delivery metrics.


**Preferred / Nice\-to\-Have**
* Experience in technical environments (software development/cloud/data projects).
* Exposure to Scrum of Scrums or scaled Agile environments.
* Certifications such as CSM, PSM, or SAFe.
* Experience with reporting tools (Power BI, Excel dashboards).
* Ability to support basic technical discussions with engineering teams.
