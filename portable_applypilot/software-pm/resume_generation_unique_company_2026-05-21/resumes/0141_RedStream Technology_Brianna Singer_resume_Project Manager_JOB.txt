**Project Manager\- Technology Implementation** 




*Remote, Contract Role* 




*West Coast Hours* 




  





 Our client is seeking a Project Manager\- Technology Implementation, to support the successful delivery of technology programs and projects across enterprise clients.
 



  





 This role is designed for early\-career professionals who are building a foundation in program delivery while developing the skills to manage increasingly complex workstreams and client engagements.
 



  





 We are looking for someone who can bring structure, organization, and clarity to delivery teams while learning how modern programs operate in a fast\-paced, technology\-driven environment.
 



  





 The ideal candidate uses AI, automation, and modern tools to improve efficiency, accuracy, and delivery operations.
 



  





 They will collaborate with program managers, engineers, and clients to ensure coordinated, transparent execution aligned with project goals.
 



  





**Key Responsibilities** 



* Manage day\-to\-day execution of assigned projects or workstreams, ensuring tasks, milestones, and deliverables are completed on time and within scope.
* Maintain program artifacts including status updates, workback schedules, sprint boards, and documentation with a focus on accuracy and clarity.
* Facilitate team and client meetings, capture key decisions, and ensure follow\-through on action items.
* Track risks, issues, and dependencies, proactively surfacing and escalating as needed.
* Collaborate with engineering, product, and client teams to coordinate activities and maintain alignment across workstreams.
* Apply standard delivery methodologies (Agile, Waterfall, or Hybrid) to support consistent and structured execution.
* Leverage AI and modern tooling to improve personal productivity, automate routine tasks, and enhance the quality of reporting and communication.
* Continuously build program management, delivery, and consulting skills through hands\-on experience and mentorship.



  





**Qualifications** 



* Bachelor’s degree
* 1–3 years of experience in project coordination, PMO support, or delivery execution
* Foundational understanding of program management concepts such as timelines, risk tracking, and status reporting
* Strong organizational, communication, and problem\-solving skills
* Ability to manage multiple tasks and priorities in a fast\-paced environment
* Experience with collaboration and delivery tools such as Microsoft Office, Teams, SharePoint, Azure DevOps, or Jira



  





**Advantageous skills** 



* Experience supporting technology implementation or software delivery projects
* Exposure to Agile or hybrid delivery methodologies
* SCRUM Master certified
* Familiarity with AI\-assisted productivity tools (e.g., Copilot or similar)
* Management Consulting, Technology Consulting or client\-facing experience
