Job Description
Summary: The Technical Delivery Manager III leads the execution of high-impact, strategic technology initiatives, ensuring coordination across platforms and teams. This role is responsible for end-to-end delivery management, including planning, resource alignment, risk mitigation, and stakeholder engagement. The Technical Delivery Manager builds strong partnerships with technical teams, product and vendors, driving accountability and customer satisfaction. They provide technical aptitude, mentor team members, and proactively manage scope, schedule, budget, dependencies, and escalations. Additionally, they champion delivery best practices, continuous improvement, and operational excellence through retrospectives and performance metrics.
Company Overview: Envision is a leading national medical group focused on delivering high-quality care to patients when and where they need it most. You’ll find clinicians and clinical support professionals across the nation who are proud to call Envision home. We welcome teammates of every background and work in communities that reflect the racial, ethnic, gender, sexual orientation, and economic diversity of our country.

Responsibilities
- Leads end‑to‑end delivery of large‑scale, complex, and strategically significant technical initiatives, applying deep subject‑matter expertise in integrations, cloud infrastructure, and enterprise platform implementations.
- Builds, strengthens, and maintains partnerships with technical teams, vendors, and cross‑functional stakeholders to ensure alignment and seamless execution.
- Owns stakeholder engagement and communication, ensuring transparency and alignment throughout the project lifecycle.
- Proactively identifies and mitigates risks, issues, and dependencies, ensuring timely resolution and escalation as necessary.
- Develops and maintains project plans, schedules, and budgets, ensuring adherence to timelines and financial targets.
- Champions continuous improvement initiatives, leveraging retrospectives and performance metrics to drive operational excellence.
- Mentors and develops team members, fostering a culture of collaboration, accountability, and high performance.
Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
- 5+ years of experience in technical delivery management, preferably in a healthcare or technology environment.
- Strong understanding of ITIL principles and practices.
- Proven experience managing complex technical projects with cross-functional teams.
- Excellent communication, leadership, and interpersonal skills.
- Ability to work in a fast-paced, dynamic environment and manage multiple priorities.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Project Management Professional (PMP) certification is a plus.
