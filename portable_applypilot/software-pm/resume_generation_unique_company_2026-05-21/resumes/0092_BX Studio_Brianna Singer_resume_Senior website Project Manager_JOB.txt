Senior Website Project Manager
Location: Remote
Company: BX Studio
About BX Studio
BX Studio is a digital agency specializing in website design, development, and optimization. We partner with clients in diverse sectors to create high-performing, visually compelling websites that drive results. Our service model is tuned for rapid delivery, building on Webflow, Framer and AI stacks. Our team is committed to delivering exceptional quality and maintaining long-term client relationships through ongoing strategic, creative and technical support. We pride ourselves on our collaborative culture, technical expertise, and dedication to staying at the forefront of web development best practices. BX Studio was recognized as Webflow Enterprise Partner of the Year 2025.
The Role
We are seeking an experienced, detail-oriented Website Project Manager to oversee and coordinate website projects. This role focuses on managing the client experience, coordinating the work of our design and development teams, and ensuring timely, on-budget project completion. The ideal candidate has experience in managing multiple projects, has a strong website project management background, and excels in communication and team coordination.
Key Responsibilities
- Client Communication: Act as the primary point of contact for clients, addressing their website needs, structuring their participation, gathering requirements, and setting clear expectations.
- Task Management: Assign tasks to the design, development and QA teams, ensuring that all project milestones are met and that the project stays on track.
- Quality Assurance: Oversee the quality of deliverables, ensuring that they meet client expectations and industry standards.
- Budget Management: Monitor project budgets, ensuring that projects are delivered on time and within budget.
- Reporting: Provide regular updates to clients and internal stakeholders on project status, risks, and issues.
Requirements
- Proven experience as a Project Manager in a digital agency or similar environment.
- Strong understanding of website design and development processes.
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
- Ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously.
- Proficiency in project management tools and software.
- Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
If you are passionate about delivering exceptional website projects and thrive in a collaborative environment, we would love to hear from you!
