Job Description**Summary of position/daily activities:**   
We are seeking highly motivated **Deputy Program Managers** to join our team and play a pivotal role in advancing federal program delivery and operational excellence across complex, multi\-stakeholder environments. AEM Deputy Program Managers understand program management, system integration, and service delivery, and support the translation of strategic objectives into coordinated execution across technical and functional teams. In this position, you will oversee day\-to\-day operations, manage cross\-functional coordination, and ensure alignment between program priorities, technical delivery, and customer expectations. You will support planning, risk management, and performance tracking while ensuring quality standards, timelines, and service levels are consistently met. You will play an integral role in driving collaboration, maintaining continuity of operations, and enabling continuous improvement across program activities.

* Support the Program Manager in overseeing day\-to\-day operations of a Federal Agency enterprise digital services program supporting multiple mission applications and stakeholder communities
* Coordinate activities across engineering, data, UX, infrastructure, cybersecurity, and operational support teams to maintain consistent delivery of program services
* Monitor program performance including schedule, deliverables, staffing coordination, and operational metrics
* Facilitate collaboration between product owners, engineering teams, and stakeholders to ensure mission requirements are reflected in development priorities and operational workflows
* Assist with Agile delivery coordination including backlog planning, sprint cadence support, and cross team communication
* Support program governance activities including reporting, risk tracking, issue resolution, and performance monitoring
* Ensure operational processes and workflows are documented and maintained to support efficient program execution
* Assist with coordination between contractor teams and Federal Agency stakeholders to maintain alignment across program activities
