Job Title: Product Manager
Join a transformative data and AI platform initiative aimed at modernizing enterprise-scale capabilities and enabling real-time decision-making. This project delivers a comprehensive roadmap covering AI, MLOps, data governance, and platform scalability, supporting a shift towards data-first operations and intelligent automation.
What you will do
Own and manage the product backlog, ensuring it is well-defined, prioritized, and aligned with business objectives and stakeholder needs
Lead requirements elicitation and collaboration with stakeholders using advanced techniques (workshops, interviews, discovery sessions)
Translate business needs into clear, structured requirements (user stories, acceptance criteria, non-functional requirements) ensuring readiness for development
Define and communicate the product vision, roadmap, and release plans, aligning across engineering and business teams
Drive requirements prioritization using value-driven approaches, balancing customer impact, technical feasibility, and business goals
Ensure end-to-end requirements traceability, maintaining consistency across features, releases, and implementation artifacts
Collaborate closely with engineering teams to define solution approaches, clarify requirements, and support design decisions
Act as a bridge between business and technical teams, ensuring mutual understanding and alignment
Validate delivered solutions against business needs through acceptance criteria, demos, and UAT participation
Monitor and evaluate product performance metrics, identifying gaps between expected and actual value and driving improvements
Support solution validation and continuous improvement, including defect triage, feedback analysis, and backlog adjustments
Identify and manage risks, dependencies, and scope changes, ensuring transparency and proactive mitigation
Maintain clear and structured product documentation (e.g., PRDs, backlog artifacts, Confluence pages)
Contribute to process improvements in product management, requirements practices, and cross-team collaboration
