**About The Role**
 The
 **Implementation Project Manager (IPM)** 
 leads Attentive’s most complex enterprise customer implementations project that often involve multiple business units, global rollouts, data integrations, and cross\-functional coordination across Product, Engineering, and Services.
   

  

 This role blends
 **project management discipline, technical fluency, and executive stakeholder management** 
 . You will own the structure and cadence of the implementation: defining milestones, managing dependencies, surfacing risks early, and ensuring every internal and customer team remains aligned to the delivery plan.
   

  

 Your goal: to deliver on\-time, high\-quality launches \& migrations that set customers up for long\-term success on Attentive’s platform.
   

  

**What You’ll Accomplish** 
  

  

* Project Leadership
+ Lead enterprise\-scale, multi\-product implementation projects across SMS, Email, Push, and AI \- often spanning multiple geographies and customer brands.
+ Create and own detailed project plans, timelines, and governance structures that bring order and predictability to complex customer launches.
+ Drive accountability across Attentive and customer stakeholders, ensuring scope, timelines, and deliverables are clearly defined and met.
+ Manage cross\-functional execution across Implementation, Solutions Architecture, Product, Engineering, and Customer Success.
+ Anticipate risks, escalate effectively, and develop mitigation plans that keep projects moving forward without compromising quality.


  

  

* Customer \& Stakeholder Management
+ Serve as the single point of project accountability for enterprise customer implementations \- representing Attentive’s delivery commitments to senior customer stakeholders.
+ Build trusted relationships with customer project managers, technical leads, and executives.
+ Lead joint steering committees, status updates, and executive reviews to maintain transparency and confidence throughout the implementation.
+ Align internal leaders across Revenue, Product, and Services to ensure decisions are made quickly and in the best interest of customer success.


  

  

* Technical \& Operational Coordination
+ Partner with Solutions Architects to ensure technical requirements, data integrations, and platform configurations are correctly scoped and implemented.
+ Coordinate dependencies across internal product surfaces (e.g., data pipelines, APIs, message delivery systems) and external customer or partner platforms.
+ Track project metrics \- including implementation velocity, defect rates, launch readiness, and customer satisfaction to measure and improve delivery performance.
+ Capture learnings from each project and contribute to the evolution of Attentive’s implementation playbooks and methodologies.


**Your Expertise**
* 6\+ years of experience managing large\-scale implementation or technical delivery projects for enterprise SaaS or martech customers.
* Proven success leading multi\-workstream projects across technical, operational, and business domains.
* Strong technical fluency in areas such as data integrations, APIs, and platform configuration \- able to translate complex requirements into actionable plans.
* Exceptional communication and executive presence \- capable of engaging senior customers and internal stakeholders with clarity and confidence.
* Expertise in building governance models, risk frameworks, and communication cadences for enterprise projects.
* Experience with project management tools (e.g., Asana, Rocketlane, Jira, Smartsheet) and CRM systems (e.g., Salesforce).
* PMP, PMP, or comparable certification is a plus.
