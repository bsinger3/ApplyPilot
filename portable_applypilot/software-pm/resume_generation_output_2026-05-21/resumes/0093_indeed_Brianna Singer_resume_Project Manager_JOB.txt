**Job Summary**


The Project Manager is responsible for owning the end\-to\-end delivery of low\-complexity, multi\-channel projects. This role ensures that assigned projects are delivered on time, within scope, and within budget. The Project Manager serves as the primary day\-to\-day lead for project execution, managing timelines, deliverables, and approvals while collaborating across teams to ensure smooth delivery.


**Job Responsibilities and Skills**


* Manage the full lifecycle of assigned projects, ensuring they are delivered on time, within scope, and within budget.
* Serve as the primary point of contact for day\-to\-day project communication, including status updates and deliverable reviews.
* Maintain timelines, assignments, and trackers in Workfront, Egnyte, Gantt charts, and related tools.
* Partner with Account and Finance teams on budget creation, scope tracking, and compliance (including Med/Legal requirements).
* Ensure deliverables move through the full review and approval lifecycle accurately and efficiently.
* Build trust through consistent and reliable execution, occasionally sharing project updates with clients.
* Proactively identify risks, escalate issues as needed, and recommend solutions.
* Contribute to process improvements and propose enhancements to increase efficiency and quality.
* Mentor more junior team members (e.g., Project Coordinators) when applicable.


**Minimum Qualifications**


* 2\+ years’ experience in a pharmaceutical advertising or digital agency
* 2\+ year of project management experience preferably in pharmaceutical commercialization and launch
* Passionate about working in a team environment
* Experience collaborating and communicating with cross\-functional teams
* Ability to be effective in remote work via Teams etc. with internal teammates, stakeholders, clients, etc.
* Has financial understanding of project and accounts
* Great communicator and leader; and able to build rapport with people quickly and easily
* Strong time management skills
* Ability to work independently and in a team environment
* Ability to prioritize and consistently handle multiple tasks across multiple clients and teams
* Ability to handle changing priorities while meeting deadlines
* Willingness and ability to learn
* Ability to keep a cool head and positive attitude when executing tasks across accounts
