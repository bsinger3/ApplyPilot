**Job Summary**
 Are you a strategic thinker with a strong technological background, passionate about making an impact through AI\-driven solutions? We are seeking a Technical Project Manager to oversee the comprehensive delivery of innovative SaaS projects in a dynamic, global environment.
   

  

**What are the responsibilities for this role?**
* Lead medium to large\-scale technical projects from conception to completion.
* Define project scope, establish timelines, and ensure adherence to project schedules.
* Collaborate with cross\-functional teams to bring AI\-driven solutions to fruition.
* Manage client delivery, including SOW validation, API integrations, AI model training, and more.
* Facilitate Agile/Scrum ceremonies and advocate for best practices.
* Serve as the liaison between technical teams and stakeholders, translating complex concepts into clear communication.
* Proactively identify risks and lead with effective problem\-solving strategies.


**Requirements**
* Minimum of 3 years in technical project management, preferably within a SaaS and AI/chatbot context.
* Proficient in tools such as Jira, with a solid understanding of SDLC, API integrations, and AI model evaluation.
* Proficient with technologies such as Replit, Claude Code, Flowise, Make, or similar AI tools.
* Fluent in English, with the ability to lead discussions among multiple stakeholders.
* Highly organized, tech\-savvy, solutions\-oriented, and ready to take initiative.


 Nice to Have
   

  

* Experience in the CPG, Retail, or Banking sectors.
* Background in Product Management or proficiency in Python.
* Skills in Power BI, Looker, AWS, or Google Cloud.
* Fluency in Spanish is a plus!
