About the job

Lead Global Tech Projects That Drive Business Systems and Innovation

 

As a Technical Project Manager, you will translate business goals into technical initiatives and manage the planning, execution, and delivery of system projects including CRM, Order-to-Cash, and Finance platforms. You will coordinate teams, track progress, manage risks, and ensure successful project delivery across technical and business stakeholders.

This role plays a critical part in ensuring technology initiatives are delivered on time, aligned with business goals, and implemented successfully across global teams. You will build a long-term global career with Emapta while working with international stakeholders in a structured and growth-focused environment designed for top talent.




Job Overview:




Employment type: Indefinite term type contract

Shift: Day Shift, Weekends Off

Work setup: Work From Home/Remote

The Qualifications We Seek




3-5 years of experience in IT project management or business systems management within a SaaS, technology, or healthcare environment
Hands-on experience with Salesforce (CRM) and/or financial systems such as NetSuite, including supporting enhancements or integrations
Understanding of system integrations and automation tools (e.g., Workato) and exposure to analytics platforms like Snowflake or Tableau
Experience coordinating cross-functional projects and managing multiple stakeholders in a fast-paced environment
Strong communication skills with the ability to translate technical concepts into clear business language
Detail-oriented problem solver with strong analytical skills and proactive mindset
Familiarity with Agile methodologies, release cycles, and change management principles
Proficiency in project planning tools such as Jira, Asana, Smartsheet, or Microsoft Project




Your Daily Tasks




Translate business goals into actionable technical tasks and define success metrics for project delivery
Manage planning, execution, and delivery of CRM, Order-to-Cash, and Finance system initiatives
Track project progress, coordinate timelines, and ensure business readiness for system launches
Partner with technical and business stakeholders to align priorities and manage project dependencies
Maintain clear communication across teams to ensure project alignment and transparency
Support project reporting, steering committee updates, and system demonstrations
Assist with testing, release planning, and post-launch evaluations during system rollouts
Identify project risks early and contribute to mitigation planning strategies
Support effective allocation of team resources to maintain project momentum




About the Client




Our client operates in the healthcare technology and software industry, providing digital platforms and business systems that help organizations improve operations, streamline workflows, and enhance service delivery. Their technology solutions support business operations, financial systems, and customer management platforms used by organizations globally. Joining this team means working on impactful technology projects that support innovation, system improvements, and digital transformation initiatives across international teams.
